The device comes with two kinds of flow cards:

- **The app's own cards** for the room, zones, air quality and firmware, listed below.
- **Homey's standard cards** for the standard capabilities on the device, such as *Presence turned on* or *The
  CO₂ level changed*. You get these automatically. Every value of the device is also available as a token, so
  you can compare it in a *Logic* card.

The complete list with every argument and token is in the [flow card reference](/docs/reference/flow-cards).

## When…

| Card | Fires when | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| **Someone became present** | The room goes from empty to occupied. | *People* |
| **The room became empty** | Nobody is left in the room (after the presence hold time). | |
| **Number of people changed** | The people count changes. | *People*, *Previous* |
| **Air quality changed** | The [air quality class](/docs/air-quality) changes, for example from *Good* to *Lightly polluted*. | *Air quality*, *Previous* |
| **Someone entered [zone]** | The zone goes from empty to occupied. | *People* (at least 1) |
| **[zone] became empty** | Nobody is left in the zone (after the zone's hold time). | |
| **Movement started in [zone]** | Someone in the zone starts moving. | |
| **Movement stopped in [zone]** | Movement in the zone stops. | |
| **New firmware is available** | Sensy-One released a firmware newer than the sensor runs. Fires once per version. | *Version*, *Installed version* |

<Callout type="info" title="No false triggers after a reconnect">

When the sensor reconnects it re-sends all its values. The app does not treat those as changes, so a
reconnect never fires *someone became present*, *zone entered* or similar cards. Zone cards also never fire
for zones that have no outline.

</Callout>

## And…

| Card | True when |
|---|---|
| **Someone is / isn't present** | The room is occupied (or not). |
| **[zone] is / isn't occupied** | Someone is inside the zone (or not). Always false for a zone without an outline. |
| **There is / is no movement in [zone]** | Someone moves inside the zone (or not). |
| **Number of people is / isn't above [count]** | The people count is higher than *count* (0–3). |
| **Air quality is / isn't [level] or worse** | The air is at least as polluted as *level*. False until the sensor reported a class. |

## Then…

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Set presence hold time of [zone] to [seconds] seconds** | Changes the zone's hold time on the sensor (0–3600 s). Handy to make a zone "stickier" at night. |
| **Beep for [duration] seconds** | Sounds the buzzer for 0.1 to 5 seconds. Pitch and volume come from the [buzzer settings](/docs/settings#buzzer). |

## Examples

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Light on when someone sits down at the desk">

- **When** *Someone entered* **Zone 1 (desk)**
- **And** a *Logic* card: the device's *Luminance* token is less than 50
- **Then** turn on the desk lamp

And a second flow: **When** *Zone 1 became empty* **Then** turn off the desk lamp. Raise the zone's
*presence hold time* if the lamp goes off while you are still reading.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Ventilation on high CO₂">

- **When** *The CO₂ level changed* (Homey's standard card)
- **And** a *Logic* card: the *CO₂* token is greater than 1000
- **Then** set the ventilation to high

With a separate flow back to normal below 800 ppm, so it does not switch on and off around one value.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Feed the sensor's readings to a ventilation system">

Many ventilation systems accept external CO₂ and humidity values. Use **When** *The CO₂ level changed*
**Then** *report the CO₂ level* with the device's *CO₂* token, and the same for humidity.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Bathroom light that stays on in the shower">

- **When** *Someone became present* **Then** bathroom light on.
- **When** *The room became empty* **Then** bathroom light off.
- In the [device settings](/docs/settings#detection), set *Presence hold time* to a few minutes; the radar sees
  people in the shower, but water and glass can make detection patchy.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Warn when the air gets bad">

- **When** *Air quality changed*
- **And** *Air quality is* **Moderately polluted** *or worse*
- **Then** send a notification: "Air quality is now [Air quality], open a window."

Right after the sensor started, the air quality is not calibrated yet; see
[Air quality](/docs/air-quality#calibration). You can check the *Air quality calibration* token in a *Logic*
card if you want to skip notifications until it shows *Calibrated*.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Night mode for a bedroom">

- At 23:00: **Set presence hold time of Zone 1 (bed) to 1800 seconds**.
- At 07:00: set it back to 120 seconds.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Know when the sensor has new firmware">

**When** *New firmware is available* **Then** send a notification: "Sensy firmware [Version] is out
(installed: [Installed version])."

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Tips

- **Use zones rather than the room** when only part of a room matters; zone cards ignore what happens elsewhere.
- **Tune the hold times** before building complex flows; most "light goes off while I sit still" issues are a
  hold time that is too short.
- **Combine with luminance**: the sensor's own light reading tells you whether a light is needed.
