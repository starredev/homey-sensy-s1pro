Sensy S1 Pro turns the [Sensy-One S1 Pro Multi Sense](https://github.com/sensy-one/S1-Pro-Multi-Sense) into a
native Homey device: presence that also sees people who sit still, up to three zones, the number of people in the
room, and CO₂, air quality, temperature, humidity, pressure, light and UV.

Homey talks to the sensor directly over the ESPHome native API on your local network. No Home Assistant, no MQTT
broker and no cloud.

## What it does

<Cards>
  <Card title="Presence and people" href="/docs/device">
    Presence that stays on while you sit still, movement, and how many people are in the room.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zones" href="/docs/zones">
    Draw up to three zones on a live radar map, each with its own presence, flows and settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Climate and air quality" href="/docs/air-quality">
    CO₂, an air quality index with a plain-language class, VOC, temperature, humidity, light and UV.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Radar widget" href="/docs/widget">
    A live top-down radar of the room on your dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flows" href="/docs/flows">
    Triggers, conditions and actions for the room, every zone, air quality and new firmware.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" href="/docs/settings">
    Every setting of the sensor, kept in sync in both directions.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Get started

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check the requirements

A Homey Pro (2023) or Homey Pro mini on Homey 13 or newer, and the S1 Pro on the official firmware.
[Requirements](/docs/installation#requirements)

</Step>
<Step>

### Install the app and add the sensor

Install **Sensy S1 Pro**, then add the sensor under **Devices → + → Sensy S1 Pro**.
[Installation](/docs/installation)

</Step>
<Step>

### Draw your zones

Open **Apps → Sensy S1 Pro → Configure** and draw zones while you walk through the room.
[Zones](/docs/zones)

</Step>
<Step>

### Automate

Build [flows](/docs/flows) and add the [radar widget](/docs/widget) to a dashboard.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

The app is a Homey **Python** app built on
[`homey-esphomedriver`](https://github.com/Doekse/homey-esphomedriver), the shared ESPHome layer that also powers
the generic ESPHome app for Homey. That library handles discovery, pairing, encryption, reconnects and the
standard capabilities. This app adds what is specific to the S1 Pro: zones, live targets, flow cards, the
settings mirror, the zone editor, the radar widget and firmware notices.

Everything runs on your local network. The only outbound request is a check for new firmware on Sensy-One's
GitHub releases page, every six hours.
