Flows

The app's triggers, conditions and actions, with ready-to-use recipes.

The device comes with two kinds of flow cards:

  • The app's own cards for the room, zones, air quality and firmware, listed below.
  • Homey's standard cards for the standard capabilities on the device, such as Presence turned on or The CO₂ level changed. You get these automatically. Every value of the device is also available as a token, so you can compare it in a Logic card.

The complete list with every argument and token is in the flow card reference.

No false triggers after a reconnect

When the sensor reconnects it re-sends all its values. The app does not treat those as changes, so a reconnect never fires someone became present, zone entered or similar cards. Zone cards also never fire for zones that have no outline.

Examples

  • When Someone entered Zone 1 (desk)
  • And a Logic card: the device's Luminance token is less than 50
  • Then turn on the desk lamp

And a second flow: When Zone 1 became empty Then turn off the desk lamp. Raise the zone's presence hold time if the lamp goes off while you are still reading.

  • When The CO₂ level changed (Homey's standard card)
  • And a Logic card: the CO₂ token is greater than 1000
  • Then set the ventilation to high

With a separate flow back to normal below 800 ppm, so it does not switch on and off around one value.

Many ventilation systems accept external CO₂ and humidity values. Use When The CO₂ level changed Then report the CO₂ level with the device's CO₂ token, and the same for humidity.

  • When Someone became present Then bathroom light on.
  • When The room became empty Then bathroom light off.
  • In the device settings, set Presence hold time to a few minutes; the radar sees people in the shower, but water and glass can make detection patchy.

  • When Air quality changed
  • And Air quality is Moderately polluted or worse
  • Then send a notification: "Air quality is now [Air quality], open a window."

Right after the sensor started, the air quality is not calibrated yet; see Air quality. You can check the Air quality calibration token in a Logic card if you want to skip notifications until it shows Calibrated.

  • At 23:00: Set presence hold time of Zone 1 (bed) to 1800 seconds.
  • At 07:00: set it back to 120 seconds.

When New firmware is available Then send a notification: "Sensy firmware [Version] is out (installed: [Installed version])."

Tips

  • Use zones rather than the room when only part of a room matters; zone cards ignore what happens elsewhere.
  • Tune the hold times before building complex flows; most "light goes off while I sit still" issues are a hold time that is too short.
  • Combine with luminance: the sensor's own light reading tells you whether a light is needed.
