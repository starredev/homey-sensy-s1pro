The device

What the device tile shows, and where every value comes from.

The S1 Pro Multi Sense device is a Homey sensor. Its tile shows the room's presence and climate; zones appear as extra values for every zone you draw.

Presence and people

ValueCapabilityMeaning
Presencealarm_presenceSomeone is in the room, also when they sit or lie still. Stays on for the presence hold time after the last detection.
Motionalarm_motionSomeone in the room is moving.
Peoplesensy_peopleNumber of people the radar tracks, 0 to 3.

Homey's zone activity reacts to both presence and motion sensors, so the room the device is in becomes active as soon as the sensor sees someone, and stays active while they sit still.

Up to three people

The radar tracks at most three targets at a time. With more people in the room the count stays at 3.

The air quality values are explained in detail on the Air quality page.

How often these values update depends on Real-time environment readings: on (the default) they follow every change, off they update once a minute.

Zones

For every zone you draw in the zone editor, three values appear:

ValueCapabilityMeaning
Zone N presencesensy_zone_presence.zoneNSomeone is inside zone N
Zone N movementsensy_zone_movement.zoneNSomeone moves inside zone N
People in zone Nsensy_zone_people.zoneNNumber of people inside zone N

They appear a few seconds after you save a zone with at least three points, and disappear again when you clear the zone. Zones you never drew do not clutter the tile.

Maintenance actions

Open the device and look under maintenance (the wrench, or Maintenance in the device settings) for:

  • Calibrate CO₂ sensor
  • Restart sensor
  • Restart radar
  • Refresh capabilities

See Maintenance and firmware for what they do and when to use them.

Insights

Presence, motion, people, all climate values and the zone values are logged in Homey Insights, so you can see when a room was used and how CO₂ or humidity developed over the day. The air quality class and its calibration state are text values and are not logged.

Warnings on the device

The device can show a warning bar with one or more of these messages:

WarningWhat to do
The BLE proxy is on…Turn off BLE | Proxy on the sensor's web page; see Requirements.
Firmware vX is available…A newer official firmware is out; see Firmware updates.

When the sensor is offline

If the sensor stops answering, the device becomes unavailable with the message ESPHome connection lost. The app reconnects by itself as soon as the sensor is back, also when it got a new IP address. Flows do not fire for values that are merely re-sent after a reconnect.
