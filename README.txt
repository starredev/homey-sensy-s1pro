Turn the Sensy-One S1 Pro Multi Sense into a native Homey device: mmWave presence that also detects people sitting still, up to three zones with their own flows, the number of people in the room, and air quality, CO₂, temperature, humidity, pressure, light and UV.

The app talks to the sensor directly over the ESPHome native API on your local network. No Home Assistant and no MQTT broker are needed. Draw zones in the built-in zone editor while you watch people move on a live radar map, and add the Sensy radar widget to a dashboard.

Unofficial community app; not made or endorsed by Sensy-One. Works with the official Sensy-One firmware.
