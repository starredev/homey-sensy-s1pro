The **S1 Pro Multi Sense** device is a Homey *sensor*. Its tile shows the room's presence and climate; zones
appear as extra values for every zone you draw.

## Presence and people

| Value | Capability | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Presence** | `alarm_presence` | Someone is in the room, also when they sit or lie still. Stays on for the [presence hold time](/docs/settings#detection) after the last detection. |
| **Motion** | `alarm_motion` | Someone in the room is moving. |
| **People** | `sensy_people` | Number of people the radar tracks, 0 to 3. |

Homey's **zone activity** reacts to both presence and motion sensors, so the room the device is in becomes
active as soon as the sensor sees someone, and stays active while they sit still.

<Callout type="info" title="Up to three people">

The radar tracks at most three targets at a time. With more people in the room the count stays at 3.

</Callout>

## Climate and air

| Value | Capability | Source on the sensor | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Temperature** | `measure_temperature` | BME688 | °C |
| **Humidity** | `measure_humidity` | BME688 | % |
| **Pressure** | `measure_pressure` | BME688 | mbar (= hPa) |
| **CO₂** | `measure_co2` | SCD40 (a real CO₂ sensor) | ppm |
| **Air quality (IAQ)** | `sensy_iaq` | BME688 / Bosch BSEC | 0–500 index |
| **Air quality** | `sensy_air_quality` | BME688 / Bosch BSEC | *Excellent* … *Extremely polluted* |
| **Air quality calibration** | `sensy_iaq_accuracy` | BME688 / Bosch BSEC | *Stabilizing* … *Calibrated* |
| **VOC** | `measure_tvoc` | BME688 / Bosch BSEC | ppm |
| **Luminance** | `measure_luminance` | LTR390 | lx |
| **Ultraviolet** | `measure_ultraviolet` | LTR390 | UV index |

The air quality values are explained in detail on the [Air quality](/docs/air-quality) page.

How often these values update depends on [*Real-time environment readings*](/docs/settings#detection): on (the
default) they follow every change, off they update once a minute.

## Zones

For every zone you draw in the [zone editor](/docs/zones), three values appear:

| Value | Capability | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Zone N presence** | `sensy_zone_presence.zoneN` | Someone is inside zone N |
| **Zone N movement** | `sensy_zone_movement.zoneN` | Someone moves inside zone N |
| **People in zone N** | `sensy_zone_people.zoneN` | Number of people inside zone N |

They appear a few seconds after you save a zone with at least three points, and disappear again when you clear
the zone. Zones you never drew do not clutter the tile.

## Maintenance actions

Open the device and look under maintenance (the wrench, or *Maintenance* in the device settings) for:

- **Calibrate CO₂ sensor**
- **Restart sensor**
- **Restart radar**
- **Refresh capabilities**

See [Maintenance and firmware](/docs/maintenance) for what they do and when to use them.

## Insights

Presence, motion, people, all climate values and the zone values are logged in Homey **Insights**, so you can
see when a room was used and how CO₂ or humidity developed over the day. The air quality class and its
calibration state are text values and are not logged.

## Warnings on the device

The device can show a warning bar with one or more of these messages:

| Warning | What to do |
|---|---|
| *The BLE proxy is on…* | Turn off **BLE \| Proxy** on the sensor's web page; see [Requirements](/docs/installation#bluetooth-proxy). |
| *Firmware vX is available…* | A newer official firmware is out; see [Firmware updates](/docs/maintenance#firmware-updates). |

## When the sensor is offline

If the sensor stops answering, the device becomes **unavailable** with the message *ESPHome connection lost*.
The app reconnects by itself as soon as the sensor is back, also when it got a new IP address. Flows do not fire
for values that are merely re-sent after a reconnect.
